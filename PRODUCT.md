# Product

## Register

product

## Users

StockSentinel serves individual investors doing Chinese-language equity research. It must welcome people who know a company name but not its code or quantitative terminology, while preserving enough control for experienced users to test and compare their own ideas.

## Product Purpose

Make real market data usable for personal research, from monitoring through chart reading, screening, backtesting, portfolio review, and risk analysis. A successful screen helps a user understand what a test will do before running it, then lets them inspect evidence rather than treating results as investment advice.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, credible. The product communicates in direct Chinese, explains consequences before jargon, and keeps financial information precise without pretending to predict the market.

## Anti-references

- Dense trading-terminal layouts that expose every control before a user has chosen a task.
- Stimulus-heavy stock apps that use urgent color, flashing motion, or promotional copy to manufacture confidence.
- Generic dashboard decoration that competes with research decisions.

## Design Principles

1. Start with the investor's intent, then reveal the quantitative implementation.
2. Let people use company names, pinyin, and codes interchangeably.
3. Make the next action and its outcome explicit before asking for configuration.
4. Preserve expert controls through progressive disclosure instead of a separate product.
5. Treat data, uncertainty, failures, and costs as information to explain plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and controls. Provide visible keyboard focus, labels and errors that do not depend on color alone, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion-safe transitions. Chinese labels should explain specialist terms when they first appear.
